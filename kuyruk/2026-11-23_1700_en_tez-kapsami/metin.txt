Thesis topic: set 3 boundaries. An ambitious topic needs a workable boundary. If you are balancing research with teaching, paid work or caring responsibilities, begin with the project you can realistically run. Use page 1 of the free planner to separate the topic, scope and research question. The example in this Reel is illustrative: defining a setting and available material is a planning move, not proof that a design is sound. Check access, permissions and method fit with your supervisor or advisor. AI can suggest alternatives, but you need to explain the final choice.
